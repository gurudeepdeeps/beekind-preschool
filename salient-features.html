Salient Features

Our Unique Features

The key highlights that shape a joyful, disciplined, and value-driven learning experience.

Learning a joyful experience

Laying a strong foundation for future learning by providing maximum opportunity for perception, observation, auditory and visual experience, the basic needs for concept formation.

Personal Attention

Giving maximum personal attention to each student in our school. Few ways: Publish tasks, Strategic Seating, Effective Grouping, Peer Recognition.

Periodic Evaluation

Evaluating periodically the students through tests and assignments, and supporting parents by identifying areas for improvement.

Encouragement

Encouraging students and instilling confidence in them to take part in all co-curricular activities.

Maximum Opportunities

Providing maximum opportunities to get trained in various fields and paving the way for holistic development of personality.

Moral and Cultural Values

Inculcating moral and cultural values in children, imbibing secularism and communal harmony right from childhood to develop a broader vision.
