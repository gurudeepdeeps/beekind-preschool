Boarding

"A home away from home"

In a warm, cheerful, and carefully supervised environment, BeeKind Preschool provides boarding support that helps children feel safe, settled, and cared for beyond regular school hours. Our spaces are designed to feel familiar and comforting while still encouraging learning, play, rest, and independence.

Boarding at BeeKind plays an important role in supporting the overall development of children while parents are away at work. Every child is guided by attentive staff who care for emotional comfort, healthy routines, hygiene, safety, meals, rest, recreation, and meaningful engagement. The goal is to create a second home where children grow with confidence, kindness, discipline, and joy.

Food menu for the boarders

Beverages
  • Milk, warm water, lemon drink, buttermilk, fruit juice, or seasonal healthy drinks.
Breakfast
  • South Indian: Idli, dosa, upma, pongal, avalakki, vegetable bath, or sambar rice.
  • Light options: Bread with spread, fruits, porridge, or cereal depending on the day's plan.
  • Sides: Chutney, sambar, vegetable gravy, or curd-based accompaniments.
Lunch
  • Rice, chapati, vegetable curry, dal, curd, rasam, salad, and seasonal vegetables.
  • Menus are planned to keep meals balanced, child-friendly, and hygienic.
Dinner
Vegetarian:
  • Chapati, rice, dal, vegetable kurma, paneer or mixed vegetable gravy, curd, papad, and occasional sweets.
  • Food is served in a calm routine that helps children practise table manners and independent eating habits.
Special menu:
  • Festival meals, seasonal fruits, simple snacks, and celebration treats are served on special occasions.
