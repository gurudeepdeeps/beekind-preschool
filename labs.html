Labs

Physics Lab

'To see is to believe, to do is to know.'

Our Physics lab introduces children to early science through observation, sorting, matching, simple experiments, and guided questioning. Children are encouraged to wonder, predict, try, and talk about what they see around them.

Chemistry Lab

'Science is simply common sense at its best.'

The chemistry lab gives children space to observe reactions, mix substances, explore changes, and understand cause and effect. These hands-on activities support observation skills, curiosity, and early scientific reasoning.

Biology Lab

'To nurture a gardener is to feed not just the body, but the soul.'

The biology lab gives children space to explore nature, plants, animals, life-cycles, and the living world. Through observation, simple projects, and guided activities, children develop curiosity, respect for life, and early biological understanding.

Computer Lab

'Learning is the only thing the mind never exhausts, never fears, and never regrets.'

Our Computer Lab nurtures curiosity, problem-solving, and age-appropriate digital skills through guided activities, educational tools, and playful learning experiences.
