News & Events

Admissions open for the academic year 2026-27

Join BeeKind Preschool for joyful early learning with caring teachers, playful classrooms, and activity-based growth. Applications are open for Pre-Nursery, Nursery, LKG, and UKG. Limited seats available, secure your child's brighter start today!

Teachers Recruitment 2026-27

Teacher recruitment is open for the academic year 2026-27. Interested candidates can contact the school office with their updated resume and recent passport size photo.

Inter House Competition

Our little learners participated with confidence in creative competitions, music, movement, and theme-based activities that encouraged expression and teamwork.

Gurupurnima Celebration

Gurupurnima was celebrated with songs, gratitude activities, and simple classroom rituals that helped children understand respect for teachers, parents, and elders.

Aksharabhyasa

Aksharabhyasa was held in a traditional and child-friendly manner, introducing young learners to the joy of letters and learning.

Investiture Ceremony

The Investiture Ceremony celebrated confidence, responsibility, and leadership through simple roles and classroom participation.

Exhibition

Children displayed craft work, classroom projects, and creative learning activities, giving parents a warm glimpse into their progress.

Orange Day and Rakshabandhan Celebration

Orange Day and Rakshabandhan were celebrated with vibrant dress-up, craft work, festive songs, and joyful classroom bonding.
