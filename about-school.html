All about the school

At BeeKind Preschool, we believe that early childhood is the most important stage in a child’s learning journey. Our goal is to create a warm, safe, and joyful environment where children feel confident to explore, discover, and grow every day.

We follow a child-centered learning approach that encourages curiosity, creativity, communication, and social development through engaging activities and play-based learning.

Our Unique Features

Playgroup

Designed for children aged 1.5–3 years, our Playgroup programme focuses on sensory play, social bonding, and early language development.

Nursery & LKG

Structured yet playful sessions that introduce early literacy, numeracy, and creative arts through hands-on exploration.

UKG

A school-readiness programme preparing children with phonics, maths concepts, and life skills in a stimulating classroom environment.

Arts & Music

From drawing and craft to singing and rhythm, we nurture every child's creative expression through dedicated arts & music classes.

Sports & Yoga

Age-appropriate physical education, outdoor play, and mindfulness sessions that promote health, balance, and focus from an early age.

Boarding

Safe, warm, and stimulating boarding support for working parents, with nutritious meals, nap time, and enrichment activities.
